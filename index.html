Pietro Capovilla

I am a PostDoc at the University of Regensburg, Germany, in the research group of Clara Löh, sponsored by the SFB 1785.
Previously, I was a PostDoc at the University of Cyprus in the research group of Christoforos Neofytidis.
I did my PhD at Scuola Normale Superiore, in Pisa, under the supervision of Roberto Frigerio.

I am interested in geometry and topology.
